Ride the Waves Retreat 2027

January 15–17, 2027 · The Beach House, Hilton Head Island, SC

A weekend of recovery, fellowship, learning, and fun by the sea. Our theme this year is Back to the Basics and How to Apply Them.

This year, we're going back to the basics, and learning how to apply them. Together, we'll revisit the Steps and the foundations of recovery, then explore how we put these timeless principles into practice in our relationships, conversations, boundaries, sponsorship, service, and everyday life. Come discover new ways to live your recovery, not just work it.
